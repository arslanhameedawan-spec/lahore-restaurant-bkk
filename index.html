Our Story

Born from faith, resilience and a love of Pakistani food.

Lahore Restaurant began during the COVID-19 period, when businesses across Thailand were facing difficult times.

Our family, already active through Hira Fashion Accessories Plaza in Sampheng and Al-Barakah Goat Farm, saw a need for genuinely authentic halal Pakistani food in Bangkok.

With trust in Allah and a desire to share Pakistan’s traditional flavours and hospitality, Lahore Restaurant opened its doors.

The opening was inaugurated by H.E. Sahebzada Ahmed Khan, then Ambassador of Pakistan to Thailand, with members of the Thai-Pakistan business community and distinguished guests.
